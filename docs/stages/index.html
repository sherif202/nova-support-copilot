Decisions I took, and why

DecisionChosenWhy
Model for both stepsclaude-haiku-5-5, low effortCheapest and fastest; Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 cost 10 to 50 times more and scored no better (stage 3 bake-off).
Handoff thresholdKeep 0.7Labels at 0.60 to 0.69 were right only 13 of 32 times; at 0.6 a declined card abroad would get card-replacement advice.
VendorVendor-neutral adapter, Anthropic for the pilot onlyProduction depends on data residency, CBE rules and the bank's contracts. Switching is a settings change (docs/MODEL-STRATEGY.md).
AutomationAuto-answer low-risk, with Off and Suggest-only modesLow-risk questions only; Suggest-only is the shadow and beta mode for stage 7.
Product namesA product catalogue plus taxonomy rows, names onlyCustomers ask by product name; no product facts exist in the policies, so the copilot hands off instead of inventing rates.
