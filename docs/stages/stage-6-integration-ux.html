The 7 ticket states

  • resolved_pending_confirmation answered by the AI, waiting for "Did this solve it?"
  • moved_to_agent waiting for an agent
  • moved_to_fraud with the Fraud team
  • draft_pending_approval Suggest-only: an AI draft waiting for review
  • approved reviewed and sent to the customer
  • resolved_by_customer_choice the customer picked an option and got an answer
  • closed closed by an agent (undo within 10 seconds)

Mapping to the blueprint's state names: to confirm with the blueprint owner.

The three UX pillars and the Telos critique

PillarBuilt
1 · Trust & reliabilityCalibration card in the customer's language; plain-words confidence badge on every reply; an honest one-line reason and a person on every handoff
2 · Explainability"Why this answer?": general → sources in plain words with icons → decision; codes kept out of the answer; readable labels in the inbox
3 · Human agency"Did you mean…", block-card confirm, "Did this solve your question?", automation modes, agent edit-and-approve, relabel and close with undo

The screens were then critiqued against Nova's Key Results with the Telos method. Its two top recommendations are applied: the feedback question is now "Did this solve your question?", the exact signal the North Star counts, and every handoff carries a one-line summary so the agent never asks the customer to repeat.
