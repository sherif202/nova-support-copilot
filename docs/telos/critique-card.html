Does each screen serve Nova's goals?

Telos critiques a flow against a Key Result, not against generic UX rules: an alignment score, what already serves the goal, what to reconsider ranked by impact, numbered suggestions, and the sub-metric each one moves. Then the critique itself is scored before anyone trusts it. Applied here by hand to Nova's own screens and goals; the plugin itself was not installed.

Recommendations that keep updating

The Telos idea is turned into a feature: the PM control panel recomputes its recommendations from live numbers every time it is opened, each tied to a Key Result, ranked HIGH or MEDIUM, and tagged with its sub-metric. For example: fraud tickets waiting (KR2), "not sure" handoffs over 10% (KR1), cost over EGP 0.035 per answer (KR3), p95 over 4 s (KR4), the busiest queue as the bottleneck (KR1). This card is the one-off critique; the panel is the critique that never goes stale.
