Nova Bank support copilot · project map · 6 Nov 2026

Everything built, tested and written, on one page

An AI copilot that answers three repeat banking questions (fees, disputes, cards) in Arabic, English and Franco-Arabic, from the customer's own records, and hands everything else to a person. Built along a 9-stage lifecycle. Nova Bank is fictional and all data is synthetic.

How the parts connect

The copilot

A fixed 7-step workflow. The AI is used at only 2 steps; code checks everything around it.

1 · Mask personal datacode
2 · Fraud check firstcode
3 · Understand the questionAI
4 · Look up own records + rulescode
5 · Write the answerAI
6 · Check every number & wordcode
7 · Reply, ask, or hand to a personcode

◎What the customer and agent get

  • Chat in Arabic, English and Franco, with "Why this answer?"
  • Pickers: tap the exact transaction, card or dispute
  • Transaction investigator: why pending or declined, "waiting for money"
  • Chat memory and one clarifying question (1.0.14 and 1.0.15)
  • Agent inbox with a handoff summary; modes Off / Suggest / Auto
Stage 6Stage 4

⛨Guardrails

  • Fraud goes first, to a person, with no AI
  • No source, no answer: no guessed amounts or dates
  • Never says "approved", "refunded" or "waived"
  • Only the logged-in customer's data (Banking Law 194/2020)
  • Card numbers masked; 20 messages a minute per customer
Stage 1Stage 5

✓Proof so far

  • Release test 106 cases: fraud 13/13, security 18/18, intent 96.9%, grounded 97.1%
  • Fresh handoff test: 2 of 24 to a person
  • Shadow run: 63% answered (an answer rate, not resolution), 0 of 16 fraud missed

Results vary between runs. Replies at the slow end take 5.5 s (limit 6 s).

Stage 5Stage 7

£Cost and business case

  • About EGP 0.02 per answer; capacity about 370 to 3,100 messages a minute
  • ROI, base case: +18% if hiring falls, −56% on minutes alone
  • Break-even: 1,824 to 4,869 resolved contacts a month
  • The first figure of 9,485% was wrong and is withdrawn
