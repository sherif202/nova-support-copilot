Catch

Label what should be retrieved, not only the answer

So retrieval can be measured before generation

Nova's copilot doesn't search a vector index in the MVP. It retrieves in three simpler ways, and each one gets its own label and its own metric. Measuring them before the answer is written tells you whether a wrong answer came from fetching the wrong evidence or from writing badly.

What is retrievedHowLabel on each caseMetricTarget
Customer recordsCode ranks this customer's transactions, disputes and cards by match to the amount, date and merchant in the message, and passes the top 3expected_records, in orderRecall@3 and MRR (is the right row first?)≥ 95% · ≥ 0.9

Worked example · case 1

Message 3ayez a3raf el 25 geneh elly et5asamo yom 14/9 Expected records: TXN-2026-09-14-0087 rules: FEE-2.3 Lookup top 3 (no model call yet) 1 TXN-2026-09-14-0087 Fee: other-bank ATM -25 ✓ 2 TXN-2026-09-14-0086 ATM, other bank -500 3 TXN-2026-09-01-0003 Card yearly fee -120 Recall@3 = 1 of 1 right rows found = 100% Precision@3 = 1 of 3 rows are right = 33% MRR = right row is first = 1.0 Citations = [FEE-2.3] vs [FEE-2.3] = P 100%, R 100%

Why these metrics

Precision@3 is low by design here. When only one row is right, the best possible score is 33%. So the gate uses Recall@3 and MRR for records, and saves precision for citations, where extra rules really do mean a worse answer.

Retrieval runs without the model. A lookup-only run of all 60 cases costs nothing and takes seconds, so it runs on every change.

"None" is a label too. For "How much is a cheque book?" the right retrieval is nothing. Citing any fee rule is a fail.

New source

Maintenance and outage notices

Where the AI learns "the app is down Thursday 8 to 9 pm"

One notice, three channels. Digital Operations or Card Operations publish each window once, in data/service-status.json. The SMS, the app banner and the copilot all read the same notice, so the chat never says something different from the SMS (rule DIG-9).

Live data, not policy. Notices change daily, so they are read fresh on every message, never cached in the prompt like the policies. Only notices that are happening now or later today are shown to the model; expired ones are left out in code.

No notice, no claim. If nothing is listed, the copilot says there is no announced maintenance and offers a colleague. It never guesses a cause or a fix time (DIG-8). Fraud still wins during maintenance.

Real bank later: the file is replaced by the bank's status page or SMS campaign system, read-only.

Do

Source, clean, label

Step 1 · SourceWhere each row comes from
  • Records: draft customers, transactions and disputes with Claude in the brief's shape, then check each one by hand. Every fee row must match a fee rule.
  • Tickets: 200 synthetic contact-center messages, written to match the 60% split: about 45 fees, 40 disputes and 35 cards (120, the 60%), plus 80 other contacts.
  • Real phrasing: ask 5 Egyptian friends how they'd ask each question on WhatsApp. Keep their exact words and typos.
  • Never real customer data. Test card numbers only, such as 4111 1111 1111 1111.
Step 2 · CleanMake it consistent and safe
  • One date format (YYYY-MM-DD) and one currency (EGP, negative for money out).
  • Record IDs follow one pattern: TXN-2026-09-14-0087, D-2026-0091.
  • Mask any 12+ digit number to its last 4 before saving.
  • Policies split by rule ID, never by character count, so a fee and its minimum stay together.
  • Two policy lines disagree? The stricter one wins and the conflict goes to compliance.
Step 3 · LabelTag every ticket and case
  • Label with the taxonomy: category, intent, priority.
  • Two people label the same 50 tickets separately. Target: 90%+ agreement on category.
  • Where they disagree, fix the definition in the taxonomy, not the label.
  • Save in one sheet: each labelled ticket is also a training example if the classifier ever needs fine-tuning.
Labelling guide

Decide in this order

  1. Any fraud signal? Lost or stolen card, a payment they didn't make, someone asked for a code, can't log in plus changes. → P0, stop.
  2. A complaint, a threat, or a vulnerable customer? → P1.
  3. Asks for an action the copilot can't do? Waive, refund, transfer, loan, advice, another person's account, open a dispute. → P3.
  4. One of the 3 in-scope questions? Fee, dispute status or documents, damaged or expiring card. → P2.
  5. Anything else, greetings, "talk to someone". → P4.

A higher priority always wins. A message with a fee question and a payment they didn't make is P0.

Catch

Why the eval set comes first

It outlives the modelModels change every few months. The 60 cases and their right answers are what let you swap Haiku for GPT, Gemini or something newer and know in 10 minutes whether it's better. tools/model-check.mjs runs them today.
It defines "good"Writing the benchmark answer for "Why the 75?" forces the decision on what a good reply says before anyone tunes a prompt to sound good.
It stops shipping on vibesWithout it, a demo that works on 5 friendly questions looks ready. With it, fraud recall 100% and Harmless 1.00 are numbers, not feelings.
